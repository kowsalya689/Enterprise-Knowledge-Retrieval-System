1. Welcome to Nimbus Industries

Welcome to Nimbus Industries Pvt. Ltd. This guide helps you settle in during your first 90 days. It explains what happens before you join, what to expect on day one and how to find help. Nimbus Industries is a fictional company used for training and research.

  • Our mission: to make dependable industrial components for customers across India.
  • Our values: Safety first, Honest communication, Customer focus, Continuous learning.
  • Head office: Chennai. Regional offices: Coimbatore, Madurai, Bengaluru.

Every new joiner is assigned a buddy, a colleague who answers everyday questions about the office, tools and culture for the first 3 months.

4. IT Access and Equipment

On day 1 you will receive a Company laptop, an email account, access to the HRMS portal and the chat tool. You must set up multi-factor authentication before leaving the IT Service Desk. Your password must have at least 12 characters, as described in the Information Security Policy.

  • Never share your password with anyone, including the IT team.
  • Install software only from the approved software catalogue.
  • Report a lost laptop to the IT Service Desk within 1 hour.
  • For help, write to servicedesk@nimbus-industries.example or call extension 4444.

6. Pay, Benefits and Leave

Salary is paid on the last working day of each month. Payslips are available on the HRMS portal. You are covered by group health insurance of INR 5,00,000 from the first day, and you may add your spouse, children and parents.

Leave rules are in the Leave and Attendance Policy. In short, you receive 18 days of annual leave, 8 days of casual leave and 10 days of sick leave each year, and you apply for leave on the HRMS portal. Annual leave can be taken only after confirmation.

7. Probation and Performance

The probation period is 6 months. Your manager will hold check-in meetings at the end of the first, third and fifth months, and HR confirms your employment after a positive review. Performance is reviewed each year in March, and goals are set on the HRMS portal within the first 30 days.

9. Workplace Essentials

  • Working hours are 09:30 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, with core hours from 10:30 to 16:00.
  • Wear your ID card at all times. Visitors must be registered at reception.
  • The cafeteria is open from 12:00 to 14:30; tea and coffee are available all day.
  • In an emergency, call the security desk at extension 100 and follow the evacuation signs to the assembly point in the east car park.
  • Expense claims are submitted on the Expense portal within 15 days after the expense is incurred.
